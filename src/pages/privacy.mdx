## Information you share

If you email me, I receive your name, email address, and whatever you write. I use it only to reply and continue the conversation you started.

## Analytics and cookies

This site does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies. Your browser stores one preference, your light or dark theme choice, and it never leaves your device.

## Hosting and embedded content

The hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and the pages requested, to keep the site secure and running. Videos from YouTube or Vimeo load only when you press play, and then follow those services' privacy policies.
