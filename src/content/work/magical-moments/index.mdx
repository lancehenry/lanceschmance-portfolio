Henderson sends clients a holiday card every year. In a digital-first world, the team wanted something more memorable, a message clients would actually stop and watch.

## The challenge

- Thank clients for the relationship in a way that felt fresh, not generic.
- Use motion and storytelling to lift a traditional year-end message.
- Match the brand's tone: professional but personable, thoughtful but polished.
- Work across email, social, and internal channels.

## My approach

**Concept.** I pitched several story directions with moodboards. The chosen one centered on warmth, connection, and gratitude, grounded in Henderson's brand.

**Storyboard and design.** I mapped every scene and transition, using clean illustration, festive but on-brand color, and subtle seasonal details.

**Animation.** I built the piece in After Effects with kinetic type, branded iconography, and smooth transitions, paying close attention to pacing so the message landed.

**Delivery.** I worked with internal communications on script, voiceover, and timing, then exported versions for each channel.

## The result

Clients and internal stakeholders responded warmly, and the video performed well across email and social. It became a personal touchpoint in a season full of generic greetings.
