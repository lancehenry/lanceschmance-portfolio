Henderson sends clients a holiday card every year. In a digital-first world, the team wanted something more memorable, a message clients would actually stop and watch.

## The challenge

Thank clients in a way that felt fresh, not generic, lift a traditional year-end message with motion and storytelling, and stay true to a brand that's professional but personable.

## From pitch to storyboard

I pitched several story directions with moodboards. The chosen one centered on warmth, connection, and gratitude.
