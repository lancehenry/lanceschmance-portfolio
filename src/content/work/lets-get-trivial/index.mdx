Let's Get Trivial needed a brand as fun as its events: bold, a little quirky, and easy to spot in a crowded local event scene.

## The challenge

One identity had to work across print, digital, and live events, appeal to a casual, social crowd, adapt to very different venues, and grow into merch and social content.

I started with research into other trivia and entertainment brands to find the gaps, then ran a brand personality workshop with the team to pin down tone, values, and what gets people out on a weeknight.

## The identity

The mark is a lightbulb with a human profile inside it, the moment an answer clicks, wrapped in an arched wordmark. The core kit is black and white, so it prints cleanly on anything from a coaster to a clipboard and never fights a venue's own branding. On screens, a hit of neon green turns the volume up.

Type is set in Proxima Nova: heavy, all-caps headlines with a wink, and clean text weights for rules and fine print, readable across a loud room.

## Applications

The voice does as much work as the mark. Table tents lead with lines like "No phones. Only brains." and "In trivia we trust," with a QR code to join the next game.

## The result

Flyers became more eye-catching, social engagement went up, and several venues asked for co-branded promo materials.

Next up for the brand: a website for schedules and sign-ups, a merch launch with tees and pins, social templates and motion graphics, and ready-to-go event kits for new venues and hosts.
