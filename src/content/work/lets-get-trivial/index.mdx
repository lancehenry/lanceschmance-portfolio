Let's Get Trivial needed a brand as fun as its events: bold, a little quirky, and easy to spot in a crowded local event scene.

## The challenge

- **Cohesion:** one identity that works across print, digital, and event materials.
- **Audience:** appeal to a casual, social crowd and stand out from other event nights.
- **Scale:** assets that can grow into merch, social content, and more.
- **Versatility:** branding that adapts to very different venues and formats.

## My approach

I researched other entertainment and trivia brands to find gaps, then ran a brand personality workshop with the team to define their tone, values, and what motivates their players.

From there I designed a playful custom wordmark, an energetic palette, and a type system that stays readable from a phone screen to a sign across a noisy bar. Then I rolled it out across event signage, table tents, social templates, and merch concepts like coasters and sweatshirts.

## The result

The brand gave the company a professional, recognizable identity. Flyers became more eye-catching, social engagement went up, and several venues asked for custom co-branded promo materials.

## What's next

A branded website with schedules and sign-ups, merch for players and hosts, motion graphics for social, and ready-to-go event kits for new venues.
