The Locations section is where clients go to find out where the firm works and who to call. It was static, dated, and hard to navigate.

## The challenge

- Modernize the section and make it easier to use.
- Help clients reach the right team with fewer clicks.
- Make it easy to add new offices and keep details current.
- Meet mobile and accessibility best practices.

## My approach

**Discovery.** I audited the existing pages, reviewed how other AEC and client-facing firms handle locations, and studied traffic patterns and navigation paths.

**Design.** I designed a modular layout and an interactive map that visitors can switch between list and region views.

**Integration.** The pages connect the CMS to the firm's CRM, so office details update automatically instead of by hand.

**Testing.** I reviewed the design with client directors and office leadership, ran informal usability tests with clients and internal users, and refined the visual consistency and micro-interactions.

## The result

Clients now reach the right contact with fewer clicks, and internal teams find it much easier to keep information up to date. Feedback after launch singled out the clarity, the interactive map, and the overall polish.

## What's next

Regional project highlights, team spotlights, and community stories, with a quarterly review of engagement to keep refining the layout.
