## Discovery

I audited the existing pages, reviewed how other AEC and client-facing firms handle locations, and studied traffic patterns and navigation paths. The goals: reach the right team in fewer clicks, make new offices easy to add, and meet mobile and accessibility best practices.

## Map and list, one system

I designed a modular layout and an interactive map that switches between list and region views. The pages connect the CMS to the firm's CRM, so office details update automatically instead of by hand.
