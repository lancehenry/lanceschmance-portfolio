I redesigned Henderson's Expertise section from the ground up, from the project data behind it to the pages on top: six sectors, 28 practices, and one system that holds them all together.

Henderson was reorganizing from a practice-based structure into broader sectors. The old Expertise pages were dated, hard to navigate, and no longer matched how the firm described itself.

## The challenge

The redesign had to explain the shift to a sector-based model, bring years of project data along with it, line up with the firm's evolving brand, work better for engineers and outside visitors alike, and scale as sectors are added or the structure changes again.

I started from what I knew about the brand and its audiences, reviewed competitor sites, then sketched wireframes for the navigation, the Expertise landing page, and templates for sector and practice pages. Each sector page pulls in related leaders, projects, news, and thought leadership, so visitors can keep exploring.

## Behind the pages

A new structure on the site only works if the data underneath matches it. Our records in Deltek, the firm's CRM, were still organized the old way, so we decided how to reorganize project data to fit the new sector and practice structure. I then remapped the records: projects, leader profiles, and thought leadership and news, so every item lined up with what visitors see on the site.

That backend work is what keeps the front end honest. With sector and practice data aligned at the source, each sector page pulls in its own leaders, projects, news, and thought leadership, and the site stays in step with Deltek as the structure changes.

## The system

I designed an interactive, accessible visual style and built a working prototype of the core screens, then walked marketing leadership, engineers, and client relationship directors through it and refined the UI from their feedback.
