Henderson was reorganizing from a practice-based structure into broader sectors. The old Expertise pages were dated, hard to navigate, and no longer matched how the firm described itself.

## The challenge

The redesign had to do four things at once:

- Explain the shift to a sector-based model clearly to clients and prospects.
- Line up with the firm's evolving brand and messaging.
- Be easier and more accessible for both internal engineers and outside visitors.
- Scale as new sectors are added or the structure changes again.

## My approach

**Discovery.** I started from what I already knew about the brand, its audiences, and its digital presence, then reviewed competitor sites and gathered visual references to balance usability with brand consistency.

**Structure.** I sketched wireframes for the new navigation, the main Expertise page, and templates for sector and practice pages. Each sector page pulls in related leaders, projects, news, and thought leadership, so visitors can keep exploring without leaving the page.

**Design and prototype.** I designed an interactive, accessible visual style and built a working prototype of the core screens: navigation, the Expertise landing page, and the sector and practice templates.

**Testing.** I walked marketing leadership, engineers, and client relationship directors through the prototype, refined the UI based on their feedback, and worked with developers to keep the build faithful to the design.
